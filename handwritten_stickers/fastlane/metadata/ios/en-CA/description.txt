Handwritten Stickers sets your text letter by letter from real handwritten glyphs and saves it as a transparent sticker you can send in any messenger.

• Over a hundred letter sets: a child's real handwriting, handwriting fonts, calligraphy, poster lettering, plus bubble, clay, gingerbread and neon.
• Presets for spacing, line height, baseline wobble, letter sizes and rotation, or save your own.
• 41 hand-drawn emoji stickers that sway gently; export the message as an animated GIF.
• Export as a transparent PNG, share it or save it to Photos.
• A grid tool straightens a photographed handwriting template.

Free: the editor, built-in presets, PNG and GIF export, emoji and joined letters. Themed font packs and saving your own presets are optional purchases.

Note: the letter sets cover the Latin alphabet, including Czech diacritics. The app interface is available in 45 languages.
