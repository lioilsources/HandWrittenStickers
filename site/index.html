Handwritten Stickers

Type a message and send it as a picture written by hand: in a child's handwriting, in calligraphy, in bubble letters, with an animated emoji on the side. The sticker is saved to your photos or shared straight to a chat.

Česky Privacy policy

Support

Something does not work, or a letter is missing? Write an issue at github.com/lioilsources/HandWrittenStickers/issues.

Purchases

Every feature of the app is free. Packs add more fonts and presets and are bought once, not as a subscription. On a new device or after a reinstall, open Packs and tap Restore purchases.

Handwritten Stickers

Napiš zprávu a pošli ji jako obrázek psaný rukou: dětským rukopisem, kaligrafií, bublinkovým písmem, s animovaným emoji po straně. Samolepka se uloží do fotek nebo ji rovnou nasdílíš do chatu.
